How it works

Three simple steps

One shared calendar and one shared list. You both see the same plan, so nothing gets missed and nobody has to keep track alone.

  2. 2

    Tell Duobot what to add

    Say what you need: a grocery item, a bill, a gift idea, a trip. Duobot puts it in the right list and on the calendar.
